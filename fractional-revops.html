What is fractional RevOps?

A fractional RevOps consultant gives you an experienced Head of Revenue Operations for a defined number of days per month, on a retainer. You get the strategy and the hands-on build — data model design, stage logic, forecast cadence, tooling integration — from someone who has done it before, embedded in your team's weekly rhythm.

It is not the same as an agency implementation project. The engagement is ongoing ownership: the revenue system keeps working because someone senior is accountable for it every week.

When it makes sense

  • Series A–C B2B SaaS, roughly 20–200 employees, where revenue tooling has outgrown the founder-built setup.
  • The forecast keeps missing and nobody fully trusts the CRM numbers presented to the board.
  • Sales, CS, and Finance run on different truths — three teams, three spreadsheets, three answers to "what's our pipeline?"
  • You can't yet justify a £90k–£120k full-time RevOps leader, but the operational drag is costing you more than that.

What a typical engagement covers

  • System-of-record architecture and governance (Salesforce, HubSpot)
  • Bowtie lifecycle diagnostics — where CR1–CR8 conversion actually breaks
  • Forecast modelling and a weekly commit cadence leadership can trust
  • Pipeline hygiene automation and deal-risk alerts
  • Revenue stack integration: Gong, Clay, ZoomInfo, billing, warehouse
  • AI agents in production — deal review, lead qualification, churn risk — see the AI transformation programme

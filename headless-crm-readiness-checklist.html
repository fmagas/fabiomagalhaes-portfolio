What's Inside

Four dimensions. Twelve questions.

Score each question 0–3. Thirty-six points available. The result tells you whether to build, pilot, or stop.

Dimension 01

System of record integrity

An agent cannot resolve a conflict it cannot see. Before anything reads your data automatically, one place has to be authoritative.

Dimension 02

Semantic layer

The most expensive failure in AI-driven revenue work is a metric that means two things. Agents inherit that ambiguity and scale it.

Dimension 03

Governance & cost control

Agentic projects get cancelled for two reasons: nobody can explain what the agent did, and nobody can explain what it cost.

Dimension 04

Integration surface

Headless means business logic is reachable without a screen. Most stacks have critical logic trapped in the UI layer.

Why now

Salesforce Headless 360 exposes every capability as an API, an MCP tool, or a CLI command — an admission that the browser is a bottleneck for AI. That shift moves the risk. When a human drove the UI, an ambiguous metric produced a confused rep who asked someone. When an agent drives the API, the same ambiguity produces confident, wrong action at machine speed — and nobody notices until the number lands in a board deck.
